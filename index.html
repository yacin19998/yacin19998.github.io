Learning that feels like play

EduPlay is an independent project that publishes free educational games for the browser. There are no accounts, no downloads and no payments — open a game and start.

Each game is built to make one subject clearer and more memorable, whether that's the flags of the world or the planets of our solar system.

Free to play

Every game runs in your browser at no cost.

Curiosity first

Games reward exploring and discovering, not memorising lists.

Works anywhere

Designed for computers, tablets and phones.
